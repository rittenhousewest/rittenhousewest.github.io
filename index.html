About

Postdoctoral fellow in particle physics. I do my best to understand elementary particles and what they get up to in a variety of environments using both analytic mathematics and computational software. I love physics so much, and the whole Universe, even though it is so big I can barely comprehend it.

Current obsession: DIQUARKS. Still. But also single-inclusive hadron production, neutron stars and heavy-ion collisions. I am a detective.

Teaching

I have taught a lot of undergraduate physics at University of California Davis and Irvine. I have the best student evaluations to prove it. By "the best" I mean from the most expressive and interesting students, and I did teach hundreds.

Nota Bene:

I would love to come give talks, if you would like to hear about quantum chromodynamics, diquarks in almost any situation but especially in nuclei, baryon/antibaryon asymmetries in colliders & fixed target experiments, antibaryon cross sections in cosmic rays or a new model of diquark transport of baryon number. I also love talking about hidden-color states of QCD and up-down no-spin diquarks as the proposed basis for ~20% of nuclear structure, and plenty of other physics I adore. Theory building, early Universe cosmology? I am your physicist.
